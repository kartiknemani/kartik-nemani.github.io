Philosophy

Research Philosophy

My research is driven by the conviction that the next generation of structural systems will not come from a single material class, but from the atomistic integration of distinct material families.

I believe in a "microstructure-by-design" approach, where we tailor interfaces at the nanoscale to solve macro-scale challenges in nuclear and aerospace engineering. By bridging the gap between 2D MXenes and traditional bulk ceramics, we can unlock properties previously thought mutually exclusive.

Teaching Philosophy

In the classroom, I strive to demystify complex concepts like thermodynamics and nanotechnology by connecting them to tangible, real-world extremes.

  • Active Learning: Utilizing tools like the MAX Calculator to let students predict material properties.
  • Inclusivity: Fostering an environment where diverse perspectives drive innovation in materials science.
  • Mentorship: Guiding students not just in technical skills, but in scientific communication and ethical research practices.
